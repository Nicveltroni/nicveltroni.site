San Francisco, CA

Context

Vilya, a Silicon Valley biotech startup, wanted to refresh its brand identity. A friend of mine working there asked me to run a quick design session and inject some ideas for their new direction. I accepted the challenge as an exercise in thinking simply and moving fast — delivering ideas in a very short amount of time without knowing much about the company I was asked to design for. The information and constraints were minimal, so the brief was open and lean.

Brief

Create a new logo draft for Vilya, including selection of typography, colors, and key visual (vilyatx.com)
